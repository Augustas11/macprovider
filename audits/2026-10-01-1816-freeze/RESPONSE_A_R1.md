## Carried notes

- Native *catalog* pool routes (no runtime source, no `pool_manifest`) are
  not fenced at final settlement. The hot path never fenced them either, and
  R015/R016 govern only loopback and pool-model routes. Extending the fence
  to them is a SPEC-042-R003/R005 policy change outside #1816.
- Deploy order: the gateway must accept `spec022-route-snapshot-v2` before
  the coordinator issues it, or pool-model finality holds
  (`invalid_settlement_policy_version`). That failure is fail-closed.
