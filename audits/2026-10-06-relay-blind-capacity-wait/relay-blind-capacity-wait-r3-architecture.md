Production defect being fixed: the relay-blind chat prelude required provider.RoutingEligible() (SlotsFree > 0) while reservation and consume used relayBlindBindable (slot-independent). A saturated provider session (Pearl: Mac Studio busy 0/8 slots) made every privacy-class chat fail with privacy_class_posture_stale (plain relay-blind: relay_blind_key_expired) right after consume, with no dispatch. The fix waits on the pinned session's existing slot queue, bounded by slotQueueDeadline and reservation expiry, then dispatches; timeout returns relay_blind_provider_unsupported (retryable). The lease converts to a consumed forwarded slot on relay start and is restored on handler return, mirroring the plaintext path.
