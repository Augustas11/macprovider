Production defect being fixed: both reservation selectors (selectRelayBlindProvider and selectPrivacyProvider) sorted providers by AssignedID and bound the first bindable one regardless of free capacity. A reservation pins one provider session, and the consume path then waits only on that provider's slot queue. Live, every private request for a model went to the same single-slot provider while another enrolled provider for that model was idle, so concurrent private requests timed out with relay_blind_provider_unsupported. The fix collects the candidates, ranks providers with a free slot not already claimed by the slot queue (RoutingEligible and not blocksProvider) ahead of busy ones, and rotates the start within each tier with an atomic counter. Busy providers stay selectable as a fallback. No gate, key, posture, settlement or wire behavior is meant to change.
