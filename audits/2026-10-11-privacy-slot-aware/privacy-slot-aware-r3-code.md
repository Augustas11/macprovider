Issue #1911 capacity items for the SPEC-049 privacy class:
1. Slot-aware private provider selection. Since #1910 the reservation selectors rank fully eligible candidates with an unclaimed free seat first, then busy ones, each tier starting at a uniformly random provider. This change also counts the requesting buyer account's own undispatched reservations (an in-memory index keyed account -> provider -> binding -> expiry, capped at 64 per account with no shared cap, a timer sweeps every account every 30 s while the index is non-empty, cleared when the chat reaches the slot wait; one account's selection through recording is serialized per account) against a provider's free seats, so one buyer's concurrent private requests spread deterministically. The index is account-scoped so no buyer's selection reflects or can be steered by another buyer's reservations. Rotation stays a random tier start (a per-model/key-class counter was rejected in the #1910 audit for cohort concentration and because it leaks a count of other buyers' reservations).
2. Fair share for private waiters. Previously a relay-blind/privacy chat waiting on its pinned session shared the plaintext FIFO slot queue, was capped at maxPending/4 (1 by default) relay-blind waiters per provider, could not enter when plaintext filled the queue, and gave up after min(slot-queue deadline 10 s, reservation expiry). Now the per-provider queue has two FIFO lanes (plaintext; pinned), separate caps (pinned = half the plaintext cap of max(4, slots_total), at least 1), and queue grants alternate between lanes while both wait (state dropped when the queue empties). A pinned waiter waits until reservation expiry (TTL <= 30 s) while the provider keeps granting seats, and gives up as relay_blind_provider_unsupported only after one slot-queue deadline with no grant on its provider.
