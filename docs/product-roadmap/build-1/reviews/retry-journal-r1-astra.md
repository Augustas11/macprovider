## M1 — An unresolved marker cannot preserve the exact prior offer after replacement

**Severity: Medium.**

**Evidence.** The addendum requires writing the envelope before submission and retaining it until confirmed withdrawal/replacement, but then permits an ordinary explicit new offer to replace it “after preserving an unresolved marker.” It does not require that marker to retain the prior complete signed envelope, define a durable two-attempt state, or prohibit replacement while the first attempt remains unresolved. The reason this addition exists is that `model_admission_status.v1` omits the original protected digests and cannot reconstruct them. In current coordinator WIP, `model_admission_retry.go:modelAdmissionOfferIdentity` hashes provider/candidate/runtime/served/catalog/discovery/evaluation/disclosure/artifact fields, and `retryMatchesCurrent` rejects any identity other than the exact current pending one. The Swift request contains those protected fields and freshness/signature metadata in `BYOMOfferSubmitRequestWire`; status carries only a subset.

**Failure sequence and consequence.** Offer A is saved and accepted by the coordinator, but its response is lost. An explicit different offer B replaces the local envelope while preserving only an unresolved marker. B is rejected, never reaches the coordinator, or has an ambiguous response. The coordinator may still have A as its current pending offer. The local journal now cannot reconstruct A, B cannot retry A, and closed status cannot recover A's missing fields. A per-candidate lock serializes the data loss but does not prevent it. This violates the promised recovery from ambiguous network results and the stated retention rule.

**Required correction.** Define an unambiguous journal state machine and retention rule. Either refuse a different new offer until the earlier attempt has been conclusively reconciled, or retain both complete exact envelopes plus attempt/generation identities until the coordinator confirms which attempt is current; do not replace recovery-critical bytes with a marker alone. Specify rejection, timeout, crash, withdrawal and delayed-response transitions, and fence stale completions so they cannot discard newer unresolved data. If multiple attempts are retained, include them in the size/count bounds and define what happens when the bound is reached without evicting unresolved attempts. Add tests for A accepted/response lost followed by B rejected, B timeout, crash around each journal transition, and a late A response racing B. Prove the retry of the actual pending tuple remains recoverable or that the new offer was blocked before any overwrite/HTTP.

## Assessment of the remaining proposal

Persisting the original validated closed envelope is the correct integration direction. The current closed status schema is insufficient to reconstruct a retry, and recomputing discovery/evaluation evidence would change the protected tuple. The addendum preserves server-side signature/current-key/tuple/state/CAS authority, requires fresh retry freshness fields, rejects revoked/withdrawn state and missing/mismatched local evidence, and does not turn a journal into admission authority.

The specified private permissions, no-symlink/regular-file checks, atomic writes, identity-derived filenames, bounded payload/count, config/test-root isolation and exclusion of bearer/private-key material are appropriate requirements. During implementation, “validated signed envelope” must include verification of the original canonical signature against the current admission public identity before re-signing; permissive `Codable` decoding and a matching key digest alone do not prove intact original bytes. The global 128-record bound must be enforced atomically across different candidate locks, and corruption must be detected before unbounded file reads. These are direct implementations of the addendum's existing validation and bounds requirements, not new scope or separate findings.

The proposed tests cover initial write-before-HTTP, exact tuple retention across restart, key/candidate/provider substitution, serialization, storage limits, filesystem failures and non-persistence of secrets. They must gain the replacement/ambiguity cases above. Fixture tests and this review do not establish physical acceptance. Correct M1 and rebind the revised addendum to a fresh independent gate; journal implementation remains paused pending that approval.
