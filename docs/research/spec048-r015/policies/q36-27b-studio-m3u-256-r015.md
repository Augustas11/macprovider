## Run plan

The matrix needs about 35 h of bench time (estimate from the 2026-10-01
exploratory 27B runs: prefill ~305 tok/s, ordinary decode ~33 tok/s at one
row, ~68 ms/step at four rows). The live provider may be paused for at most
45 minutes at a time, with at least 20 minutes of live serving between pauses,
so the campaign runs as many windows, all appending to one JSONL through the
bench's existing `--only-cell` resume:

- Each window runs `native-mtp-bench --only-cell <cell>` for one cell at a
  time. It starts a cell only when the estimated time for model load, any
  missing warmups, and one measured block fits in the remaining window.
- A window ends a cell only on a written block boundary (a measured block's
  pair is written atomically by the bench; a warmup block is never cut
  between its two records). The bench's resume skips completed blocks, so a
  cell may span windows; a block never does.
- The sustained window runs alone, after `s8-p4096-o512`'s matrix blocks
  complete, in one window.
- Nothing is excluded. Failed, incomplete, or interrupted cells stay in the
  evidence and the analyzer verdict.
