## 1. Purpose and scope

SPEC-043 is the SPEC-042A enablement profile: it defines how MacProvider may let approved external creators launch single-operator Trusted Pools on MacProvider before multi-party pool economics or Layer 3 Privacy Pools exist.

The launchable MVP is intentionally narrow:

- the creator is approved by MacProvider before pool creation;
- the creator controls the pool root issuer key and pool manifest signing authority;
- the creator brings the pool's provider supply, using already-admitted SPEC-003 provider identities;
- the creator distributes or resells access to its own buyers outside MacProvider's creator-billing product;
- MacProvider provides manifest acceptance, durable pool state, fail-closed routing isolation, pool-scoped buyer authorization, promise surfaces, and labeled settlement records;
- MacProvider does not execute creator revenue split, does not admit arbitrary third-party provider supply into the creator's pool, and does not market the pool as cryptographic privacy.

This spec is required because SPEC-042 defines the pool control plane and manifest machinery, but does not itself define the external creator onboarding, approval, root-key registration, administration, or production enablement path needed to safely make Trusted Pools live for parties outside MacProvider.

Commercial terms between the creator and MacProvider are governed by a separate Creator Agreement. SPEC-043 does not define prices, resale terms, service credits, or creator billing plans, but a valid Creator Agreement is a launch prerequisite and is referenced by the approval, activation, and audit records below.

### Explicit non-goals

SPEC-043 does not define open self-service public pool creation. Creation is approved-only until abuse, compliance, support, and sanctions workflows exist.

SPEC-043 does not define a Bittensor-style open subnet market. Multi-party pools, third-party provider opt-in, provider revenue share, creator payout accounting, sanctions appeals, and cross-pool provider reuse governance are Layer 2 V1/V2 work.

SPEC-043 does not define Privacy Pools. Coordinator-blind content, unlinkable routing, anonymous membership, nullifiers, ZK membership proofs, and unlinkable settlement are Layer 3 work owned by SPEC-041 and future specs. Buyer-facing copy MUST use "Trusted Pool" and MUST NOT use "Privacy Pool" for this MVP.

SPEC-043 does not authorize legal, medical, banking, HIPAA, attorney-client, financial-record, or other regulated-vertical compliance claims. Regulated vertical claims require a later compliance package, retention matrix completion, support process, and counsel-approved disclosures. The Creator Agreement MUST bind creators not to make prohibited claims in their own product, marketing, resale, investor, sales, or support materials during MVP.

## 2. Dependencies and authority

SPEC-043 owns `trusted-pool-creator-onboarding`: the external creator approval gate, creator root-issuer registration workflow, pool creation request, creator administration surface, production enablement checklist, and MVP operational contract for single-operator Trusted Pools.

SPEC-042 remains the owner of `pool-control-plane`: pool identity, signed manifests, policy core, authority log, pool membership/revocation ledger, pool-enforceable predicates, tenant isolation, pool status/policy surfaces, lifecycle, and route/settlement labels. SPEC-043 MUST consume SPEC-042 rather than redefining those primitives.

SPEC-003 owns provider identity. A creator may admit only provider identities already admitted by the network; SPEC-043 MUST NOT mint a second provider identity or let a creator route to an unauthenticated provider.

SPEC-006 and SPEC-040 own buyer credentials and wallet sessions. SPEC-043 MVP MAY authorize plain API-key accounts to select a pool, but wallet-session pool selection remains blocked until SPEC-040 signs `pool_id` and `manifest_core_digest`.

SPEC-005, SPEC-015, SPEC-016, and SPEC-022 own billing, receipts, payouts, and verified-model settlement. SPEC-043 MVP adds labels and auditability only; it MUST NOT claim creator split execution until SPEC-005/SPEC-016 amendments land.

SPEC-008 and SPEC-020 own trust evidence and provider release evidence. SPEC-043 MAY require only predicates that the active coordinator can enforce fail-closed from those owner specs. Predicates that are observe-only or self-reported MUST be disclosed as not enforcement-grade.

## 3. Normative requirements

**SPEC-043-R001 - Approved creator account.** A Trusted Pool creator MUST be an approved MacProvider account before any pool identity is accepted. Approval MUST record creator account id, public display name, legal/support contact, billing contact, emergency notification endpoint, acknowledged maximum response time, allowed product category, support owner, approval actor, approval timestamp, allowed launch environment, Creator Agreement id/version, Creator Agreement expiry date, Creator Agreement grace-period end date, pricing schedule id/version, prohibited-claim acknowledgment covering the full R009 enumerated claim set, buyer-disclosure commitment for downstream users, and objective approval criteria checked. The Agreement expiry date and grace-period end date MUST be first-class durable coordinator fields distinct from the Agreement id/version and MUST be sufficient for route-time and background gate evaluation without a live external lookup. The prohibited-claim acknowledgment MUST bind the creator against making prohibited claims in the creator's product, marketing, resale, investor, sales, or support materials. The buyer-disclosure commitment MUST confirm that the creator will provide end buyers with materially equivalent disclosure of MacProvider coordinator and selected-provider-operator content visibility before or at first use, with the exact disclosure mechanism defined in the Creator Agreement. The approval criteria MUST cover creator identity, sanctions/abuse screening, support readiness, commercial agreement on file, allowed use category, data-retention category, and launch environment. An expired or non-renewed Creator Agreement MUST trigger creator account suspension under the same rules as other suspensions. The Creator Agreement MUST define any grace period before suspension and the pool fate after the grace period expires; if no grace period is defined, suspension MUST occur at expiry. An expired Creator Agreement that remains before its stored grace-period end timestamp MUST satisfy the R008 valid Creator Agreement gate and MAY continue routing until grace-period end; suspension and pool unavailability take effect at grace-period end, not at Agreement expiry, unless the Agreement defines no grace period. The coordinator MUST schedule event-driven gate re-evaluation at the exact stored expiry timestamp and stored grace-period end timestamp, treating each as a creator-status change under the R003/R008 30-second bound, and MUST also re-verify Creator Agreement validity during R008 background gate sweeps. A sweep that cannot determine live registry validity due to transient error MUST retain the last known valid or invalid state and MUST NOT flip a creator to valid; after three consecutive failed checks, or any fail-closed registry error, the Agreement MUST be treated as expired until a valid durable renewal is recorded. A disabled or suspended creator MUST NOT request or consume root-registration nonces, register root issuers, retire roots, change custody disclosure, create new pools, publish new policy versions, rotate operational signer sets, submit lifecycle transitions, admit members, authorize or remove buyers, modify buyer authorization, or receive new pool-required traffic. Suspension MUST NOT disable the authenticated emergency root-compromise reporting channel defined in R002. Suspension MUST invalidate pending root-registration nonces, unconsumed registration bundles, and pending creator-admin operation ids for that creator and launch environment. If signer rotation requires publishing a new policy manifest version, this prohibition is subsumed by policy publication; if signer rotation is a distinct admin operation, it MUST be independently gated on creator-enabled status. Creator disable/suspension, including MacProvider-initiated suspension, MUST be a route-time production gate: all pools owned by that creator MUST become unavailable before quota reservation, every affected pool MUST atomically bump generation and invalidate undispatched reservations within the same 30-second event window, existing buyer authorizations MUST stop authorizing new traffic, `pool_status.json` MUST expose lifecycle `paused` with a closed reason such as `creator_suspended`, and any temporary override MUST be a signed/audited MacProvider break-glass action with expiry. Before any pool may transition to lifecycle `active` after creator re-enable, the coordinator MUST complete a full R006 re-verification sweep of every admitted member delegation credential and predicate eligibility for every pool owned by that creator; R008 reactivation MUST be gated on that sweep, and eligible member counts in the gate and `pool_status.json` MUST reflect post-sweep state.

**SPEC-043-R002 - Creator root issuer registration.** A creator MUST register exactly one stable root issuer public key per pool identity before the first policy manifest is accepted. Root-registration nonce issuance, root registration, root retirement, and custody-disclosure mutation MUST be gated on enabled creator state, current approval version, and the requested launch environment. Root-compromise reporting MUST NOT be gated on creator-enabled state, Creator Agreement validity, or launch eligibility; it MUST use a separately authenticated emergency channel that remains usable while the creator is disabled, suspended, expired, or under investigation. The registration MUST bind creator account id, current approval version, root issuer key id, root issuer public key fingerprint, root signature algorithm, proof-of-possession signature, delegated SPEC-042 manifest authority root key id, delegated SPEC-042 manifest authority root public key, structured key custody disclosure hash, genesis nonce digest, intended pool display name hash, launch environment, and approval record id. The canonical field `root_issuer_public_key_fingerprint` MUST be `SHA256(domain || u16_be_len(algorithm_id) || algorithm_id || u32_be_len(canonical_public_key_bytes) || canonical_public_key_bytes)`, where `domain` is the exact ASCII string `macprovider/spec043/root-issuer-key/v1`; the SPEC-043 root algorithm registry contains exactly one MVP `algorithm_id`, `ecdsa-p256-sha256`, encoded as ASCII bytes and using DER SubjectPublicKeyInfo public-key bytes; any additional algorithm_id or non-SPKI public-key encoding requires a SPEC-043 amendment before acceptance; and `canonical_public_key_bytes` is the single DER SubjectPublicKeyInfo encoding for MVP; key ids, JSON encoding, whitespace, and registration-envelope fields MUST NOT affect the fingerprint. The coordinator MUST reject root issuer public-key fingerprint reuse across every non-test SPEC-043 registration unless the earlier registration is the same pool identity and the operation is an idempotent replay of the already accepted bundle. The coordinator MUST maintain a durable root-fingerprint lineage index covering every registration, pool, signer set, routeable snapshot, and tombstone descended from that fingerprint, and lineage indexes plus tombstones MUST key on `root_issuer_public_key_fingerprint`. The proof-of-possession input MUST be a versioned, domain-separated canonical structure `RootKeyRegistrationV1` containing an exact published field list for every registration-bundle field except the proof-of-possession signature field, plus nonce, nonce expiry, purpose, creator account id, and environment; `RootKeyRegistrationV1` MUST be encoded as RFC 8785 canonical JSON with lexicographically sorted member names and no unknown fields; the exact signed field set is creator_account_id, current_approval_version, root_issuer_key_id, root_issuer_public_key_fingerprint, root_signature_algorithm, manifest_authority_root_key_id, manifest_authority_root_public_key, structured_key_custody_disclosure_hash, genesis_nonce_digest, intended_pool_display_name_hash, launch_environment, approval_record_id, nonce, nonce_expiry, purpose, and environment, excluding only the proof-of-possession signature field. The delegated SPEC-042 manifest authority root public key MUST be the Ed25519 root issuer key consumed by SPEC-042 authority-log and policy-history verification; the SPEC-043 P-256 root signature proves creator control of the externally registered root and binds that root to the SPEC-042 authority lineage without replacing SPEC-042's Ed25519 manifest primitives. The coordinator MUST atomically consume the one-time nonce, reject expired or purpose-mismatched nonces, verify the proof-of-possession signature over the domain-separated commitment using the submitted root issuer public key, reject the registration before persisting any bundle field if signature verification fails, and reject any registration whose authenticated creator session does not match the `creator_account_id` in the signed bundle. MacProvider MUST provide a documented root-key generation procedure or CLI that produces a registration bundle without exposing private-key material to MacProvider. The identity genesis nonce MUST contain at least 128 bits of CSPRNG entropy before hashing, so `pool_id` values are non-enumerable and cannot be guessed from creator/product names alone. `key_custody_disclosure` MUST include at least `{class, description}` where class is one of `software`, `hsm`, `mpc`, or `other`; production activation MUST require a MacProvider-approved custody class, and `software` custody is allowed only for isolated candidate or explicitly approved exception pools that are buyer-visible as such. Software-custody exceptions MUST be recorded in the creator approval record as `isolated_candidate_only` or `signed_exception_with_expiry`; a signed exception MUST include an expiry timestamp, the coordinator MUST schedule gate re-evaluation at that timestamp, and an expired exception MUST fail the R008 custody gate with reason `custody_exception_expired` until renewed or replaced by non-software custody. The root issuer private key MUST never be uploaded to MacProvider, and registration endpoints MUST reject payloads that parse as private-key material. Root issuer loss and compromise are separate cases: loss requires MacProvider-approved retirement, a MacProvider-signed continuity or non-continuity attestation, and new-pool registration with buyer-visible manual lineage signed by MacProvider but explicitly not signed by the lost root. Compromise notification MUST enter through an authenticated, rate-limited MacProvider compromise-notification intake surface; unauthenticated requests MUST be rejected before recording receipt. Every authenticated root-compromise assertion MUST trigger a provisional emergency freeze within 30 seconds of the intake surface recording an immutable receipt timestamp, and the freeze audit log entry MUST bind that timestamp; qualification and investigation MAY follow only after the freeze is active. During a root-compromise freeze, the coordinator MUST suspend creator-admin operations for every affected creator and pool, including nonce issuance, root registration, root retirement, custody mutation, policy submission, signer rotation, member admission, buyer-authorization mutation, lifecycle transition, and pool activation, rejecting them with reason `root_compromise_freeze` unless the operation is the independent MacProvider terminal-retirement path defined below. Manual break-glass is only a fallback and MUST also complete within 30 seconds of the operator receiving a paged alert. Compromise requires immutable retirement epoch/tombstone outside rollback-removable pool state, terminal retirement of the entire root-derived authority lineage, and rejection of every descendant operational signer with no exceptions from root-derived credentials; if the compromised fingerprint appears in the lineage index, freeze and terminal retirement MUST apply to every registration, pool, signer set, and snapshot descended from that fingerprint. Retirement and recovery operations during and after compromise freeze MUST use an independent MacProvider operator credential verified against the MacProvider operator credential registry, not any descendant signer set. The sole permitted retirement signer is a pre-registered MacProvider operator credential whose scope is restricted to publishing a terminal retirement attestation for the compromised root identity and co-signing the MacProvider-issued continuity or non-continuity attestation; this signer MUST NOT be co-resident with or derivable from compromised root material, MUST be pre-registered independently of the pool root key, and MUST expire when the terminal retirement attestation is issued. A root issuer MUST NOT be rotated in place.

**SPEC-043-R003 - Pool creation request.** A pool creation request MUST contain the signed SPEC-042 identity core, initial authority-log genesis entry, initial signed policy core, creator provenance fields, intended buyer disclosure, initial lifecycle state `created`, and explicit MVP mode `single_operator`. The coordinator MUST reject an `intended_buyer_disclosure` that does not state, in human-readable form, that prompts and responses are visible to the MacProvider coordinator and that selected provider operators may access request content; omission or material weakening of either statement compared with the R001 buyer-disclosure commitment MUST fail acceptance independently of R009 claim-control checks. A pool MUST NOT transition to `publicly_announced` visibility without a separate MacProvider operator approval record bound to `pool_id` and the active manifest core digest. A creation request may declare `publicly_announced` as intended mode, but the coordinator MUST hold the pool in `authorized` visibility until approval is recorded; any later transition to `publicly_announced` requires the same approval, audit-log record, and generation bump. Matching public-announcement approval is an ongoing visibility invariant: a manifest-core digest change MUST atomically downgrade visibility to `authorized` with a generation bump unless an approval for the new digest already exists and is consumed with the manifest transition. The coordinator MUST recompute `pool_id`, verify the root issuer registration, verify the authority log and initial policy, reject duplicate `pool_id` across every lifecycle state including `created`, `active`, `paused`, `draining`, `retired`, and compromised/retired lineage records, and durably record the acceptance verdict before the pool can be activated. `pool_id` values, creator root issuer registrations, buyer authorization records, and admitted provider memberships issued under SPEC-043 MUST be preserved, or receive a published migration path with migration window and backward-compatibility obligations, in any V1/V2 architecture change. A migration that eliminates, reissues, or truncates a previously issued `pool_id` without a published migration path is a spec violation regardless of whether the affected creator has a signed journey result or active pool. The valid transition from `created` to production `active` is the R008 production enablement gate; it MUST be atomic, record a gate-check id in the audit log, publish exactly one immutable routeable snapshot, and fail without partial activation if any gate item is unsatisfied. The routeable snapshot MUST bind pool generation, creator state, approval record id/version, Creator Agreement id/version, stored Agreement expiry/grace timestamps, buyer-authorization version, manifest version and core digest, membership/delegation version, lifecycle, public-announcement approval id if applicable, gate-check id, effective feature flags, and reconstruction timestamp. Post-activation gate re-evaluation MUST run within 30 seconds of any membership-ledger, delegation-ledger, predicate-state, policy, buyer-authorization, Creator Agreement time-based expiry/grace event, or creator-status change, and a background gate sweep MUST run for every pool at least every 60 seconds. Any detected gate violation MUST atomically transition the pool to lifecycle `paused` with reason `gate_violation` before the next quota reservation is accepted.

**SPEC-043-R004 - Durable coordinator state.** Pool creation MUST write durable state for identity core, root issuer registration, root-fingerprint lineage index, authority log, accepted policy records, active manifest version, lifecycle state, membership ledger, provider-owner delegation/revocation ledger, buyer authorization map, Creator Agreement id/version, Agreement expiry date, Agreement grace-period end date, routeable snapshot history retained for at least the Creator Agreement dispute window, immutable root-lineage retirement tombstones, creator provenance, and audit log. Coordinator startup reconstruction MUST be synchronous with pool capability advertisement: the coordinator MUST NOT set `pools.enabled=true` or return a positive pool-support capability until all required pool records have been reconstructed, the current routeable snapshot has been rebuilt, and its generation is current. The coordinator MUST acquire a serialization barrier after loading pool records and before finalizing any routeable snapshot so revocations, lifecycle changes, and delegation expirations written during reconstruction are included; any mutation that cannot be atomically serialized with snapshot finalization MUST force a post-reconstruction re-read of the affected ledger before the pool is marked available. Rollback, backup restore, migration, and compaction MUST NOT truncate, overwrite, or remove routeable snapshot history inside the dispute window except through a dual-operator signed audit correction that preserves the prior digest chain. Immutable root-lineage retirement tombstones MUST survive restart, restore, rollback, backup restore, migration, and compaction in an append-only or write-once logical store outside ordinary rollback-removable pool snapshots. If reconstruction finds a root-lineage retirement tombstone, descendant authority entries and snapshots from that lineage MUST remain terminally rejected across restart, restore, and rollback. If reconstruction fails globally, the coordinator MUST advertise pool support disabled. If one pool fails reconstruction, that pool MUST be individually unavailable before the coordinator accepts requests. Any pool-routed request that arrives while the pool is unreconstructed, stale, disabled, suspended, or not lifecycle `active` MUST fail closed with a pool-unavailable class error and MUST NOT retry or spill to global.

**SPEC-043-R005 - Creator administration surface.** MacProvider MUST provide a creator-admin CLI or authenticated API before launch. The surface MUST support: create pool, submit policy version, rotate operational signer set, admit provider, revoke provider, set lifecycle state, authorize buyer account or API-key scope, remove buyer authorization, fetch pool status, export a pool distribution package, fetch health events, and export audit history. Every mutating operation MUST be authenticated as the creator or a MacProvider operator acting under break-glass policy, authorized against the pool, idempotent by operation id, durably audited, and fail-closed on partial write. Creator-admin authentication authorizes access to the admin surface only; it MUST NOT substitute for SPEC-042 root, operational, or prior-threshold signatures on manifests, authority-log entries, signer rotations, or policy mutations. Break-glass authentication likewise MUST NOT create signer authority except for explicit restrictive lifecycle controls allowed by R011. Creator authentication for mutating operations MUST produce a non-repudiable audit record bound to a specific bounded-lifetime credential; permanent non-expiring creator admin credentials MUST NOT be issued. Break-glass operator authentication MUST use a distinct operator credential with RBAC, actor identity, reason code, ticket/reference id, expiry/review timestamp, and immutable audit export. All creator-admin responses, health-event payloads, distribution packages, and audit exports MUST carry a machine-readable schema version. MacProvider MUST provide advance notice before removing or breaking any operation; the notice period is defined by the Creator Agreement and MUST be at least 30 calendar days for breaking changes and 14 calendar days for strictly additive non-breaking changes, including new optional response fields, new non-required parameters, or new operation versions with prior versions fully supported. Adding a required field to an existing operation version is a breaking change. If a creator integration is not migrated by a non-breaking-change deadline, MacProvider MUST surface pre-expiry warnings in health events and audit exports but MUST NOT automatically suspend the creator unless the unmigrated integration causes a security or data-integrity failure. Security emergency exceptions require dual-operator approval and immutable audit. The migration from MVP dedicated-account buyer authorization to per-key scopes MUST be executable without creator downtime or `pool_id` reissuance, and the migration procedure MUST be documented in admin-surface release notes. For MVP, creator health events MAY be exposed by polling `pool_status.json`, but the polling interval, cache semantics, and event classes MUST be documented in the admin surface; push webhooks are V1 unless implemented.

**SPEC-043-R006 - Single-operator membership model.** MVP pool membership MUST be limited to provider identities owned by the creator account or explicitly delegated to the creator. A provider MUST NOT be auto-enrolled because it is globally admitted. Delegation MUST be a durable credential signed by the SPEC-003 provider identity owner and encoded as a canonical, domain-separated `ProviderPoolDelegationV1` envelope bound to creator account id, `pool_id`, provider identity, delegation id, operation id, active manifest core digest, environment/network id, coordinator audience, provider-owner key id/version, issuance time, expiry, and revocation semantics; a creator self-assertion is not delegation proof. The coordinator MUST maintain a durable provider-owner delegation and revocation ledger keyed by provider identity, creator account id, `pool_id`, and delegation id. Expired, owner-revoked, cross-pool-replayed, cross-environment-replayed, cross-audience-replayed, same-operation-replayed, or superseded delegations MUST be ineligible at admission and route time. Delegation operation ids MUST be globally unique across pools, creator accounts, and delegation credentials; the coordinator MUST keep a durable anti-replay log for consumed operation ids with retention at least equal to the maximum delegation expiry period declared in active pool policies and MUST NOT prune entries for delegation ids that are not fully expired or retired. Delegation expiry or owner revocation MUST immediately remove the provider from eligibility, bump pool generation, invalidate undispatched reservations, and reject replay of old operation ids; a later admission MUST require a fresh non-revoked delegation. Before pool creation, the creator MUST complete SPEC-003 provider onboarding for at least `min_eligible_members` providers in the target environment, including any required trust-tier progression for predicates in the pool policy. A provider MUST NOT be admitted to a creator pool unless the coordinator verifies ownership/delegation and the provider can satisfy every enforced predicate named by the active pool policy. The coordinator MUST re-verify delegation credentials and predicate eligibility for all admitted providers on every manifest version change. Before accepting a new manifest version, the coordinator MUST compute and surface a pre-check report listing every admitted provider whose delegation or predicate eligibility would be revoked by the new manifest core digest. If the report is non-empty, the coordinator MUST require a creator-signed dry-run acceptance token bound to the new manifest core digest and expiring within 24 hours before accepting the manifest; emergency policy updates may bypass the dry-run only through MacProvider break-glass with a dual-operator signed waiver in the audit log. Any provider whose delegation credential is bound to a superseded manifest core digest, or who cannot satisfy an enforced predicate in the new policy, MUST be revoked from the membership ledger before the new manifest version takes effect for routing; the generation bump from R011 MUST occur atomically with manifest acceptance to fence in-flight reservations. A pool with zero enforced trust predicates MUST NOT activate under the Trusted Pool MVP brand. MVP pools are `supply_mode: "shared"` unless every admitted provider is explicitly provisioned as pool-exclusive. Shared-supply pools have no throughput, latency, or capacity reservation guarantee against global traffic; the Creator Agreement MUST state that limitation, the creator MUST propagate materially equivalent downstream disclosure, and MacProvider MUST enforce the disclosure through the R001 Agreement record. A provider is pool-exclusive only if its SPEC-003 admission record carries a MacProvider-operator-set `pool_exclusive: [pool_id]` field and the provider is not simultaneously admitted to any other active pool or global routing tier. The coordinator is the source of truth for SPEC-003 `pool_exclusive`; if any global-routing admission source is external to the coordinator, unavailability or stale reads MUST be treated fail-closed as though the provider is globally admitted, blocking dedicated activation. The coordinator MUST verify both dedicated-supply conditions at provider admission, every manifest re-verification, and R008 gate time; creator-supplied delegation fields alone MUST NOT establish dedicated supply. Any claim of dedicated supply or isolated compute MUST fail acceptance unless every admitted provider is `supply_mode: "dedicated"`. Third-party provider marketplace admission and provider revenue share are out of scope for MVP.

**SPEC-043-R007 - Buyer authorization.** A buyer may select a pool only if the buyer's credential is authorized for that `pool_id`. For live external creator pools, authorization MUST be per API key or a dedicated pool-only buyer account whose disclosure and isolation are recorded in the creator admin surface and signed journey evidence; broad account-level config is acceptable only for isolated candidate tests unless this spec is amended. The storage model MUST preserve enough structure to migrate from dedicated-account MVP to per-key scopes without widening access. If per-key scope migration is not implemented in the candidate environment, the signed journey MUST record that as a blocking V1 dependency and live external pools MUST remain on dedicated pool-only buyer accounts until the migration journey is rerun successfully. `pool_id` is a non-capability identifier, not a bearer secret; the pool distribution package MUST state how authorized buyers receive it and that access is credential-bound. Unauthorized, unknown, disabled, stale, or unavailable pool selections MUST return the generic SPEC-042 `pool_unavailable` response without revealing whether the pool exists. The gateway and coordinator MUST check credential scope before any existence-dependent lookup; the gateway scope check MUST evaluate authorization for the specific requested `pool_id`, not merely that the buyer credential is globally active, and forwarding to the coordinator for per-pool authorization is prohibited when that gateway scope check rejects. The coordinator MUST provide or signal buyer-authorization generation to the gateway on every creator-admin buyer-authorization mutation, and the gateway MUST refresh buyer authorization state for the affected `pool_id` within 30 seconds; gateway cache lifetime for buyer authorization state MUST NOT exceed 30 seconds. Rejections MUST use an identical serialized negative response, status, header names and values, padded content length, cache policy, retry metadata, and lookup path across unknown, unauthorized, disabled, stale, and wallet-session-rejected pools. Implementations MUST enforce a minimum response floor using an active timing mechanism for every rejection path. The floor MUST be at least the p99 latency of authorized pool-selection requests measured at the gateway in the candidate environment and no less than 50 ms from request receipt to response-commitment-decision-to-send; the signed journey MUST record the floor value, method, and baseline distribution. Before opening live external traffic on any pool, the gateway floor MUST be re-measured in the production configuration and updated to at least the observed production p99 authorized pool-selection latency; if production p99 exceeds the candidate-derived floor, a proportional floor adjustment MUST be applied and the timing journey step MUST be rerun in the production configuration before external traffic is accepted. Optimization of any path, floor change between candidate and production, or production latency distribution change that raises authorized p99 above the configured floor MUST include proportional floor adjustment, invalidate the timing test, and require the timing journey step to be rerun. The signed journey MUST run a repeated-sample distribution test over those rejection classes and fail if the response-commitment-decision-to-send p95 delta exceeds 15 ms, p99 delta exceeds 25 ms, or a two-sample statistical test specified by the journey harness distinguishes classes at p < 0.01. Wallet sessions MUST be rejected for pool selection until a SPEC-040 amendment signs the pool binding and a SPEC-043 amendment explicitly unlocks wallet pool selection.

**SPEC-043-R011 - Lifecycle and emergency control.** Pool lifecycle transitions MUST follow SPEC-042's closed state set. Creator-initiated pause, drain, provider revocation, delegation expiry/revocation, buyer authorization removal, policy change, creator suspension including MacProvider-initiated suspension, root-key compromise, positive override expiry, on-call readiness record expiry, and gate violation MUST atomically bump pool generation and invalidate undispatched reservations. MacProvider MUST retain an operator break-glass control to pause a pool for abuse, legal, billing, safety, security, prohibited external claims, root-key compromise, or platform-integrity reasons. Break-glass is restrictive-only by default. Any positive override or creator reactivation MUST NOT bypass sanctions/legal/root-compromise holds, root or operational signatures, provider ownership/delegation, buyer scope, claim control, or the R008 production gate; it MUST require dual operator authorization, bounded TTL, automatic fail-safe expiry back to the restrictive state, generation bump, remediation record, immutable audit export, and full R008 re-evaluation before traffic resumes. Positive override expiry MUST trigger a generation bump under the same atomicity requirement as other route-affecting mutations, invalidating undispatched reservations before any quota reservation or dispatch proceeds under the post-expiry restrictive state. Re-enabling a creator MUST NOT automatically reactivate any pool. Break-glass use MUST require RBAC, actor identity, reason code, ticket/reference id, expiry or review deadline, immutable audit export, and `pool_status.json` visibility. MacProvider MUST maintain a documented on-call process capable of executing break-glass and dispatching the required notification within the Creator Agreement's notification commitment for the launch environment. MacProvider MUST store each on-call readiness record with `last_confirmed_at` and `confirmation_ttl` derived from the Creator Agreement. A readiness record approaching expiry MAY trigger pre-expiry health warnings during a Creator Agreement warning window, but when `last_confirmed_at + confirmation_ttl` is reached, the coordinator MUST atomically bump generation, invalidate undispatched reservations, transition the pool to lifecycle `paused` with reason `on_call_record_expired`, and fail closed before any quota reservation; no post-expiry routing grace period is permitted. When a pool transitions to lifecycle `paused` with reason `on_call_record_expired`, MacProvider MUST notify the creator's registered emergency endpoint within the Creator Agreement notification commitment window, subject to the same suppression rules as break-glass notification. Break-glass activation MUST notify the creator's registered emergency endpoint within that commitment, which defaults to 15 minutes unless the Creator Agreement declares a longer named exception with creator sign-off. Notification MAY be suppressed only if a court order, law-enforcement hold, or equivalent legal prohibition is in force, or if an active abuse investigation is in progress and suppression has separate RBAC approval by a second operator with `break-glass-suppress` authority. Investigation suppression MUST NOT exceed 72 hours per renewal or 168 cumulative hours without a legal hold. Each renewal requires fresh approval by an operator who approved neither the expiring renewal nor either of the two preceding renewals; at least three distinct operators MUST appear across any four consecutive renewals, and no single operator may approve more than one renewal in any continuous 72-hour suppression window. Reaching the cumulative ceiling MUST automatically notify the creator unless a legal hold applies. Legal-hold suppression MUST bind order or case id, issuing authority, scope, effective time, expiry or review deadline, and fail-safe notification behavior, and the suppression-to-legal-hold transition MUST be recorded as a distinct audit event. The immutable audit record MUST preserve the full renewal chain and distinguish `notification_sent`, `notification_suppressed_legal`, and `notification_suppressed_investigation`; creator-visible audit export MUST include the suppression category when disclosure is legally permitted. The notification MUST include a non-secret reason category and response deadline. Break-glass MUST NOT silently reroute pool-required traffic to global providers or silently route global traffic into a Trusted Pool.

## 4. Implementation, tests, and journeys

The intended first implementation is a coordinator-owned durable pool store plus a creator-admin CLI. A public self-service API may follow after the MVP proves approval, support, and abuse handling.

Suggested implementation slices:

1. Durable pool store and boot reconstruction: records for root issuer, authority log, accepted policy, membership, revocation, buyer authorization, lifecycle, and audit events.
2. Creator-admin CLI: local/operator-mediated creation and administration, using signed manifest files and idempotency keys.
3. Coordinator enablement: wire reconstructed pool state into `trustpool.Registry` and `buyer.WithPoolMembership`, advertise `pools.enabled=true` only after reconstruction succeeds.
4. Gateway authorization store: replace static `features.trusted_pools.account_pools` with DB-backed account/API-key scopes while preserving generic fail-closed errors.
5. Promise surfaces: publish closed-schema `pool_policy.json` and `pool_status.json` with claim-control rejection tests. A gateway-authenticated buyer status surface may land before public promise surfaces, but it MUST NOT be treated as public freshness enforcement or a claim-control substitute.
6. Journey harness: isolated candidate flow covering create, activate, authorize, route, settle label, revoke, pause, restart, and rollback.

The first journey id is `JOURNEY-TRUSTED-POOL-CREATOR-MVP`. Slices should be completed in order; the gateway authorization store cannot be tested end-to-end until durable pool identity records exist and coordinator pool state is wired.

## 7. Current contract notes

The MVP product name is "Trusted Pool". "SPEC-042A" is an informal product-planning label for this enablement profile; the canonical governance id is `SPEC-043` because repository tooling accepts only numeric `SPEC-NNN` ids.

The target first customer motion is design-partner launch, not open creation. A MacProvider operator approves the creator, registers or verifies the creator's root issuer public-key registration bundle, accepts the signed manifest, authorizes the creator's buyer account(s), and monitors pool status. The creator may operate its own upstream billing or resale product under the Creator Agreement; MacProvider records pool labels and can later reconcile or migrate to creator payout once SPEC-005/SPEC-016 split work exists.

MacProvider service commitments to creators, including pricing, support contacts, scheduled maintenance notice, incident response windows, schema-change notice, and dispute windows, live in the Creator Agreement referenced by R001/R008/R010. SPEC-043 enforces only the technical preconditions and audit fields needed to prove such an agreement exists for a launched pool.

`pool_id` values issued under SPEC-043 MVP are durable identifiers that MUST be preserved or receive a published migration path in any V1/V2 architecture change, with migration window and backward-compatibility obligations defined in the Creator Agreement. This commitment covers `pool_id`, creator root issuer registration, buyer authorization records, and admitted provider memberships. MacProvider MUST provide the Creator Agreement's advance notice period before any migration that requires creator action.

The launch gate is intentionally stricter than the current SPEC-042 code state. Today the repo has important primitives, a default-off coordinator reconstruction path, an operator-mediated candidate/restrictive event append path, bounded bearer-token creator administration for admitted creators, signed root registration, signed manifest acceptance with candidate overclaim rejection, coordinator-side durable buyer authorization enforcement, gateway-authenticated buyer policy/status surfaces with resolved MVP retention-policy registry fields, reviewed distribution artifact records, digest-bound public-announcement approval plus freshness-guarded public policy/status endpoints, a local operator-only candidate promotion path, and an environment-key-only CLI for event/nonce/reviewed-artifact/public-announcement/export admin workflows, but rollback-resistant production promotion authorization, production reviewed-artifact lifecycle ownership, per-key buyer-scope administration beyond dedicated-account MVP, signed journey, and unresolved SPEC-042 conformance records remain launch blockers, not follow-up polish.
