Deviations from Apple's docs, observed on real hardware:
- The attestation authenticator data has **no extensions map** (flags 0x40): no `apple_validation_category_01` and no `apple_bundle_version_01`. The verifier records this under `--expect-category any`.
- Assertion authenticator data keeps the AT flag set but is the bare 37-byte header. The verifier now parses attested credential data only when bytes follow.

Conclusion: a Malibu.app-hosted App Attest key gives the coordinator Apple-certified proof that (1) the posture signer is our team-signed `tech.malibu.app` and (2) SIP and Full Security are on.
